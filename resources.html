Possible venomous snakebite? Get medical help now

If someone may have been bitten by a venomous snake, call 911 or your local emergency service immediately. In the United States, also call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222. Do not wait for symptoms to appear.

Call 911 Call Poison Control Visit Poison Control

While help is coming

  • Move away from the snake.
  • Keep the person calm and as still as possible.
  • Remove rings, watches, and tight clothing near the bite.
  • Let emergency professionals arrange transportation when possible.
  • Take a photo only from a safe distance if it can be done without delaying medical care.

Do not

  • Do not cut the bite or attempt to suck out venom.
  • Do not apply ice or a tourniquet.
  • Do not use electric shock.
  • Do not attempt to catch, handle, or kill the snake.
  • Do not drive yourself if another option is available.

The Poison Control number above serves the United States. Outside the United States, contact your local emergency and poison services.
