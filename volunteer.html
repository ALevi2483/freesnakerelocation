Before you apply

  • Only volunteer for snakes you can identify and handle safely.
  • Have appropriate equipment, experience, and personal insurance.
  • Understand the laws, permits, transport rules, and release restrictions where you operate.
  • Obtain property-owner permission before entering private property.
  • Relocations listed through this directory must be offered without a required fee.

What the directory does not do

  • It does not send, assign, or schedule volunteers.
  • It does not promise that a volunteer will answer or be available.
  • It does not provide an availability switch that volunteers must maintain.
  • It does not monitor calls, texts, relocations, or outcomes.
  • It does not train, certify, insure, or endorse listed volunteers.

Common questions

How do I change my phone number or location?

Use the same form and choose the option that says you are already listed and need to update your information. Enter the corrected information. After admin review, the existing public listing can be updated rather than creating a duplicate.

What if I am temporarily unavailable?

You are not required to accept every request. If contacted, you may reply that you are unavailable or simply decline. The directory does not use an availability toggle because it could become outdated.

Does Free Snake Relocation send me calls?

No. People search the directory and contact volunteers directly. The administrators do not dispatch, assign, or coordinate relocations.

Do I need a permit?

Requirements vary by location and activity. You are responsible for checking and following all applicable local, state, and federal laws and permit requirements before handling or relocating wildlife.

Can I charge for a relocation?

No required fee may be charged for a relocation obtained through this free directory. Any unsolicited tip must remain optional and must be lawful where you operate.

How long does approval take?

Submissions are reviewed by volunteer administrators. Review time can vary, and submission does not guarantee publication.
