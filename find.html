Before You Use the Directory

Free Snake Relocation provides a public directory. It is not a dispatch service.

Every person listed acts independently. Directory administrators do not assign, supervise, train, certify, insure, or control volunteers. Administrators do not participate in or monitor your communications with them.

We do not guarantee that anyone will respond or be available. You decide whom to contact and whether to accept help. Use the directory at your own risk.

If you need emergency assistance or guaranteed service, contact emergency services, local animal control, wildlife authorities, or a licensed pest control provider as appropriate.

You can also read the full disclaimer.
